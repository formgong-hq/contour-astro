Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send through the contact form on this site. The site is run by [site owner], who decides how those details are used.

What the form collects

The details you type in: usually your name, your email address and your message, plus any other fields the form shows. Nothing else is collected through the form.

Why

Only to answer your message and, if you ask for it, to work with you. Your details are not used for newsletters or advertising and are not sold.

Who processes it

The form is delivered by Formgong, which receives each message, stores it and forwards it to [site owner]'s inbox. Formgong processes it on [site owner]'s behalf and does not use it for anything else.

How long it is kept

Messages are kept for as long as needed to answer them and any work that follows, and deleted after [period].

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent, or object to how they are used. Write to [contact email address]. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
